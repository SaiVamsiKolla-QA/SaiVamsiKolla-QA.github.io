Engineering approach

Four steps from intent to confidence.

A concise loop for making product risk, system behavior, and failure evidence understandable.

  1. 01

    Understand intent and product risk

    Clarify what the system must accomplish and where behavior, data, integration, accessibility, or reliability can fail.

  2. 02

    Model realistic data and failure paths

    Create valid, missing, extreme, contradictory, invalid, and stateful scenarios.

  3. 03

    Validate contracts and behavior

    Check service boundaries and deterministic results, then evaluate AI behavior against clear obligations.

  4. 04

    Diagnose failures and communicate confidence

    Connect logs, traces, browser evidence, and system state to explain failures, coverage, and remaining risk.
