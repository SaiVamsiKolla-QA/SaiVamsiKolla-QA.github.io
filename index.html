Quality-engineering approach

From intent to release confidence.

A repeatable way to make behavior, data, service boundaries, and residual risk observable.

  1. 01

    Understand intent

    Determine what the feature or system is expected to accomplish for the user and product.

  2. 02

    Identify risk

    Map behavioral, data, integration, reliability, accessibility, and user-impact risks.

  3. 03

    Model realistic data

    Create valid, missing, extreme, contradictory, and stateful data scenarios.

  4. 04

    Validate contracts

    Check schemas, service boundaries, dependencies, inputs, outputs, and failure behavior.

  5. 05

    Evaluate behavior

    Assert deterministic results and evaluate non-deterministic AI behavior against clear obligations.

  6. 06

    Trace failures

    Use logs, reports, traces, browser evidence, and system state to locate the failure point.

  7. 07

    Communicate confidence

    Explain coverage, evidence, remaining risk, and whether the product is ready.
